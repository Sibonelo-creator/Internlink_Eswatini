FAQs

Questions? Start here.

Who can use InternLink?

InternLink is designed for aspiring interns and organisations that want to share internship opportunities.

How do I apply?

Create an intern account, browse available opportunities and use the Apply button on a listing.

Can an organisation post an internship?

Yes. Create an organisation account and publish a listing from the organisation dashboard.

Are accounts and applications stored online?

This current version is a browser-based version. Its information is stored locally on the device and is not shared with other users.
